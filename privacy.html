Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who uses the App

GTA6 Clip Uploader ("the App") is used only by its operator, who connects their own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It has no public users and offers no sign-up.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok: through Login Kit and the Content Posting API, with the operator's consent: basic account info (open ID, display name, avatar) and the permission to upload and publish videos to the operator's account (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • YouTube: the permission to upload videos to the operator's channel and read its basic channel info.
  • Twitch: public clip metadata (title, view count, URL) for streamers who have given permission.

3. How data is used and stored

We use this data only to publish videos to the operator's own accounts. OAuth tokens and clip data are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never sent to anyone except the platform they belong to. We do not sell, share or rent data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track viewers.

4. Third-party processing

To generate titles and descriptions, the App sends clip transcripts (the streamer's spoken words, not personal account data) to an AI text service (Anthropic).

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google account permissions. When access is revoked, the local tokens are deleted.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
