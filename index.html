GTA6 Clip Uploader

GTA6 Clip Uploader is a private tool run by a single creator. It edits short gameplay highlights from Twitch streamers who have given explicit permission into vertical videos, and publishes them to the creator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. Each video credits the original streamer.
